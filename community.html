Community & Events

iHub Limited thrives on community collaboration. Our vibrant network includes founders, entrepreneurs, engineers, designers, and innovators from across Africa and beyond.

What We Offer

Collaborative Workspace

Modern coworking facilities in Nairobi with high-speed internet, meeting rooms, and a welcoming community of innovators.

Networking Events

Regular meetups, tech talks, and networking sessions where you can meet like-minded professionals and potential collaborators.

Mentorship & Support

Access to experienced mentors, advisors, and industry leaders who can guide your entrepreneurial journey.

Resources & Tools

Access to software licenses, development tools, cloud credits, and other resources to build and scale your ventures.

Events & Workshops

We regularly host insightful events and workshops throughout the year, including:

  • Weekly tech meetups and coding sessions
  • Startup pitch events and demo days
  • Technical workshops and training sessions
  • Industry conferences and panel discussions
  • Networking brunches and social gatherings

Contact us to learn about upcoming events or to suggest a topic you'd like to see covered.

Join Our Community

Whether you're an entrepreneur, developer, designer, investor, or innovator—there's a place for you at iHub Limited.

  • Book a Coworking Space: Day passes, monthly memberships, or dedicated desks available
  • Attend Events: Most of our events are open to the community, free or at minimal cost
  • Collaborate: Partner with our team on innovation initiatives and projects
  • Get Support: Access mentorship, guidance, and resources through our programs

Pan-African Network

As part of Africa's leading innovation ecosystem, we have connections and partnerships across the continent. Our community extends beyond Nairobi, connecting innovators in Kenya and across Africa.

Get Involved

Ready to join our community? Reach out to us to book a workspace, register for events, or discuss partnership opportunities.
