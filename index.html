Who We Are

iHub Limited is a globally recognized innovation and technology hub dedicated to accelerating the application of social capital and technology for economic prosperity across Africa. We create environments where innovators, entrepreneurs, and tech leaders collaborate to build market-relevant solutions.

As a leading member of Africa's innovation ecosystem, we work across multiple domains to empower the next generation of African leaders and creators.

What We Do

We focus on key areas that drive innovation and economic growth across the continent:

Startup Support & Incubation

We provide mentorship, resources, and acceleration programs to help startups develop and scale their solutions across African markets.

Software Development & Tech Services

Custom software solutions, cloud infrastructure, and technology consulting to drive digital transformation for organizations across sectors.

Technology & Society

We explore how technology can address social challenges and create positive impact in communities across Africa.

Education & Capacity Building

Training programs, workshops, and mentorship to build technical skills and entrepreneurial capabilities in the African tech ecosystem.

Innovation & Research

We conduct research and facilitate innovation initiatives that push the boundaries of what's possible with technology in Africa.

Creative Economy

Supporting digital creators, content producers, and creative entrepreneurs in building sustainable, thriving businesses.

Our Community & Spaces

At iHub Limited, community is at the heart of everything we do. We provide:

  • Collaborative Workspaces: Professional coworking facilities in Nairobi designed for entrepreneurs and tech professionals
  • Networking Events: Regular meetups, workshops, and conferences connecting innovators and leaders
  • Access to Resources: Tools, mentorship, and connections to help you succeed
  • Pan-African Network: Part of a continent-wide community of innovators and changemakers

Events & Learning

We regularly host events, workshops, and training sessions throughout the year. Whether you're looking to learn new skills, meet like-minded innovators, or pitch your idea—there's something for everyone.
