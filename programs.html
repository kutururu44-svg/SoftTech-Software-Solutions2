Our Programs & Initiatives

iHub Limited operates a range of programs designed to support innovation, entrepreneurship, and technology adoption across Africa.

Startup Acceleration Program

Our flagship program designed to help early-stage startups develop, validate, and scale their technology solutions. We provide:

  • Mentorship from experienced entrepreneurs and technologists
  • Access to investor networks and funding opportunities
  • Technical guidance and development support
  • Workspace and resources for product development
  • Demo days and pitch competitions

Software Development Services

We develop custom technology solutions for organizations of all sizes:

  • Web Applications: Full-stack web development using modern frameworks and best practices
  • Mobile Apps: Native and cross-platform mobile solutions for iOS and Android
  • Cloud Solutions: Scalable cloud infrastructure and migration services
  • API Development: RESTful APIs and integrations with third-party services
  • Consulting: Technology strategy and digital transformation guidance

Tech Training & Skills Development

We offer comprehensive training programs to build technical capabilities:

  • Programming bootcamps and coding courses
  • Software engineering workshops
  • Entrepreneurship and business skills training
  • Product management and design thinking
  • Cloud computing and infrastructure training

Innovation Partnerships

We collaborate with organizations, government agencies, and international partners on innovation initiatives that drive positive impact across Africa.
